Know your exam

OET or IELTS? Depends who's asking.

Every UK regulator sets its own English threshold, and the visa requirement is separate again. Here's the general shape — always confirm exact figures with your regulator before booking a test, since thresholds are reviewed periodically.

PathwayIELTS AcademicOET
Nursing & midwifery (NMC)7.0 overall, 6.5 in WritingGrade B, C+ in Writing
Allied health (HCPC)7.0 overall, no band below 6.5Accepted since 2025, profession-specific
Doctors (GMC)7.5 overall, 7.0 in each sectionGrade B in each section
Health & Care visaIELTS UKVI, B2 level from Jan 2026Can also satisfy visa if regulator threshold is met

OET is built around real clinical scenarios — referral letters, patient consultations — which tends to suit working healthcare staff better than IELTS's general topics. IELTS Academic or UKVI is usually the right call if you also need the result for a university place or a visa in one sitting.

About MedFluent Academy

Founded by clinicians. Built for people on both sides of registration.

MedFluent Academy was founded by a practising NHS paramedic based in Manchester, with a degree in Paramedic Practice from Edge Hill University, after years of teaching English alongside frontline clinical work. That clinical background shapes how the academy is run: our tutors know what NMC, HCPC, and NHS recruiters are actually listening for in an OET role-play or a registration interview, because they've sat on the clinical side of that conversation themselves.

Every tutor at MedFluent Academy is a practising or recently qualified UK healthcare professional who also teaches English — not a general language tutor working from a script.
